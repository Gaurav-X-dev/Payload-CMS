Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries regarding F&B consulting audits, operational licensing, and brand franchises.

What does a standard F&B brand audit cover?

+

A standard brand audit from Curious Laddoos covers a complete study of your restaurant's performance metrics: portion size deviations, kitchen thermal layouts, staff attrition statistics, ingredient sourcing lines, and visual menu conversions. We deliver a detailed optimization blueprint within 14 days.

How is menu engineering priced?

+

We charge a fixed, flat design and calculation fee based on the size of the menu (number of items), combined with a performance bonus linked to actual food-cost percentage reductions achieved over the first six months post-implementation.

What are the financial requirements for a Zuru Zuru franchise?

+

A standard Zuru Zuru outlet requires a carpet area of 1,800 to 2,500 sq ft and a capital investment of ₹1.2 to ₹1.8 Crores, covering kitchen equipment licensing, customized furniture elements, initial inventory buffers, and branding assets.

Do you offer area developer agreements?

+

Yes, for qualified hospitality operators, we offer multi-unit area developer agreements for specific geographic territories across South Asia. Contact hello@curiousladdoos.com to request territorial maps.

What technology stack do you deploy for kitchens?

+

Our standard tech stack includes cloud-based POS nodes, digital Kitchen Display Systems (KDS) replacing paper tickets, inventory scanners with automated stock depletion triggers, and real-time operational performance dashboards for managers.

How do you handle supply chain quality?

+

We work with national logistics partners under strict SLA conditions. All specialized ingredients (such as custom spice blends for Ghee Roast and specialized noodles/sauces for Zuru Zuru) are distributed centrally from our facilities, ensuring taste consistency across all locations.
