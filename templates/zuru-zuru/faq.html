Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to our most common questions regarding reservations, dining, delivery, and more.

Reservations

We gladly accept walk-ins, though seating is subject to availability. As we tend to get quite busy during dinner service, particularly on weekends, we strongly recommend making a reservation in advance to guarantee your table.

You can book a table up to 30 days in advance through our website reservation portal. For larger groups or special events, you can reach out to us up to 6 months prior.

We ask that you cancel at least 4 hours before your scheduled reservation time. For groups of 6 or more, a cancellation fee may apply if not cancelled within 24 hours.

Yes, we have a beautiful private dining room that can accommodate up to 20 guests. For larger parties, we also offer full or partial restaurant buyouts. Please visit our Private Dining page for more information.

Dining Experience

Our dress code is smart casual. We encourage you to dress comfortably yet stylishly. Please avoid activewear, beachwear, and flip-flops during dinner service.

Yes, children are welcome at Zuru Zuru. We offer a dedicated kids' menu and have high chairs available upon request. Please let us know if you need a high chair when making your reservation.

We offer complimentary valet parking for all our dining guests. There is also paid public parking available within a short walking distance from the restaurant.

We have a carefully curated selection of Japanese sake, wines, and signature cocktails. However, if you wish to bring a special bottle, our corkage fee is ₹1,500 per 750ml bottle, with a limit of two bottles per table.

Menu & Dietary Requirements

Absolutely. We have a dedicated section of vegetarian and vegan dishes, including our signature Mushroom Truffle Ramen and various vegetable robata skewers. Our staff can help guide you through the menu.

Yes, we offer gluten-free soy sauce (tamari) upon request and many of our dishes can be modified to be gluten-free. Please inform your server of any severe allergies before ordering, as cross-contamination is a possibility in our busy kitchen.

Our chicken and lamb are sourced from Halal-certified suppliers. However, please note that we do serve pork and alcohol on the premises, so we are not a fully Halal establishment.

Yes! Our ramen noodles are crafted fresh daily in our kitchen using specialized noodle-making machines imported directly from Japan, ensuring the perfect texture and bite.

Delivery & Takeaway

Yes, we offer delivery within a 7km radius of our location. You can order directly through our website for the best rates, or find us on premium food delivery platforms like Zomato and Swiggy.

Yes! We have specially designed packaging that separates the broth from the noodles and toppings to ensure your ramen stays fresh and doesn't get soggy on the way home.

Payments & Gift Cards

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), and cash.

Yes, digital and physical gift cards are available for purchase. You can buy digital gift cards directly from our website, or pick up a physical card at the restaurant.
