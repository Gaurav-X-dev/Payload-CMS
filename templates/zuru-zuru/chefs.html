The Visionary

Kenji Tanaka

Executive Chef & Founder

Born and raised in Osaka, the kitchen of Japan, Chef Kenji Tanaka's culinary journey began at the age of 16 in his family's humble udon shop. His insatiable curiosity led him to Tokyo, where he spent over a decade honing his skills under the watchful eyes of formidable Michelin-starred sushi masters and yakitori veterans.

For Chef Tanaka, cooking is an intimate language. He believes that true izakaya dining is not just about the food, but the energetic atmosphere, the clinking of glasses, and the shared joy of discovery. His philosophy marries rigorous traditional Japanese techniques with a playful, modern approach to flavor profiles.

At Zuru Zuru, he has curated a menu that acts as a love letter to the vibrant, chaotic, and utterly delicious alleyway eateries of his youth, bringing an uncompromising standard of excellence to every plate.

  • James Beard Foundation Award Nominee, 2023
  • Best Japanese Chef in Asia - Culinary Excellence Awards
  • Featured in "Masters of Fire: The Global Robatayaki"
Masters of the Craft

The Culinary Masters

Each master brings decades of specialized experience to ensure perfection across our diverse menu.

Akira Mori

Chef de Cuisine

With an exceptional palate and a background in French-Japanese fusion, Chef Akira orchestrates the bustling kitchen with precision. He is the mastermind behind our intricate seasonal specials and ensures every dish leaving the pass meets Chef Tanaka's exacting standards.

Haruka Suzuki

Sushi Master

Trained in Ginza for 12 years, Chef Haruka possesses an encyclopedic knowledge of seafood. Her delicate knife work and deep understanding of fish aging techniques elevate our sashimi and nigiri offerings to an art form.

Daiki Ito

Ramen Master

Chef Daiki is the guardian of our broths. Rising before dawn every day, he meticulously tends to the towering stockpots, ensuring the 24-hour pork bone and chicken broths achieve the perfect balance of richness and depth.

Yumi Yamada

Pastry Chef

Bringing a sweet conclusion to the izakaya experience, Chef Yumi expertly blends traditional Japanese ingredients like matcha, yuzu, and black sesame with classic European pastry techniques to create unforgettable, elegant desserts.

"Great food is not born from complex recipes, but from a profound respect for the ingredients and the unrelenting discipline to execute simple techniques flawlessly, every single day."
— Chef Kenji Tanaka
