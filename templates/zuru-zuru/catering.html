Catering Services

Our Expertise

Catered Events

We offer bespoke catering solutions tailored perfectly to the scale and style of your gathering.

Wedding Receptions

Make your special day unforgettable with a spectacular feast of premium sushi, elegant izakaya dishes, and live action stations that will dazzle your guests.

Corporate Events

From executive board lunches to grand gala dinners, our corporate catering ensures a sophisticated and smooth dining experience for your business associates.

Birthday Celebrations

Turn your party into a lively culinary celebration. We provide interactive food stations, customized sake menus, and vibrant flavors for all ages.

Outdoor Gatherings

Enjoy our robust yakitori grills and refreshing Japanese salads in the open air. Perfect for summer garden parties and outdoor retreats.

The Process

How It Works

1. Consultation

Contact us to discuss your event size, dietary requirements, and specific vision.

2. Tasting & Menu

Experience a private tasting session and finalize your bespoke menu selection.

3. Preparation

We coordinate logistics, staffing, and equipment with your venue managers.

4. Execution

Relax and enjoy your event as our expert team delivers a flawless culinary experience.
