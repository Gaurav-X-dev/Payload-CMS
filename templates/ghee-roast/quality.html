Our Zero-Shortcut Philosophy

The Standard We Set
For Ourselves Is Yours

At Very Good Ghee Roast, quality is not a marketing claim — it's a daily practice. From the moment our chefs arrive to hand-grind the morning masala to the final check before a plate leaves the kitchen, every action is guided by one question: "Would we serve this to our own family?"

If the answer is anything less than a confident yes, it doesn't leave the kitchen. This is not a policy — it is a culture, built over thirty years and reinforced by every chef, every morning, at every VGGR outlet.

  • Hand-sourced ingredients from named farms and regions We know exactly where every key ingredient comes from — the farm, the district, and the season.
  • No preservatives, ever Our food is cooked fresh daily. We do not use artificial preservatives, stabilisers, or shelf-life extenders of any kind.
  • 100% pure cow ghee — tested, not assumed Every batch of ghee we use is tested for purity before it enters our kitchen. No blends, no vegetable shortenings.
  • Daily hygiene audits at every outlet Our kitchens are audited every single day against FSSAI-compliant checklists. Certificates are displayed at every outlet.
  • Same masala formula since 1995 — ground fresh every morning We do not pre-mix or buy pre-ground spice blends. Our signature masala is stone-ground each morning in-house.
From Source to Plate

The Ingredients We Choose

Great food begins long before the kitchen. It begins with the grower, the region, and the decision to never compromise on what goes in.

Byadgi Chilies

Haveri District, Karnataka
Why It Matters

Byadgi chilies are prized across coastal Karnataka for their deep crimson colour, mild heat, and intensely fruity aroma. Unlike generic red chilies, they provide the rich colour of our gravy without overpowering heat — giving VGGR's Ghee Roast its distinctive look and complex, layered flavour.

Pure Cow Ghee

Local Partner Farms, Haryana
Why It Matters

Our ghee is sourced exclusively from desi cow dairy farms with whom we have long-term relationships. The ghee is traditionally churned from cultured butter, giving it a nutty, rich aroma and high smoke point ideal for slow roasting. Every batch is tested for purity. We use no refined oils — ever.

Fresh Coriander

Rajasthan & Local Markets
Why It Matters

Coriander — both the seeds and the fresh leaf — is fundamental to our masala and garnish. We source whole coriander seeds from Rajasthan, known for producing the most fragrant, oil-rich varieties in India. Fresh coriander leaves arrive daily from local markets, always same-day, never pre-cut.

How Every Day Begins

Our Daily Process

Before a single customer walks through our door, our kitchen has already been in motion for hours. This is what happens every morning — without exception.

1
6:00 AM

Morning Grinding

Our chefs arrive at 6 AM to begin grinding the base masala fresh — Byadgi chilies, coriander seeds, cumin, black pepper, and nine other spices — in our stone grinders. This cannot be done the night before. The aromatics must be fresh.

2
8:00 AM

Fresh Marination

Freshly arrived poultry and proteins are marinated in the morning-ground masala paste for a minimum of 2 hours. No pre-marinated stock, no frozen shortcuts. Every batch is prepared the day it is served.

3
10:00 AM

Slow Roasting in Pure Ghee

The marinated protein is slow-roasted in a cast-iron kadhai over a measured flame. Pure cow ghee is added in stages — not as a finishing touch, but as an active cooking medium. The roasting takes 35–45 minutes per batch. It cannot be rushed.

4
From 1 PM

Hot Packaging & Dispatch

Every order is plated or packaged immediately after cooking — never held in warming trays. For delivery orders, our insulated packaging ensures the food arrives at the same temperature it left the kitchen. No reheating. No holding. Just fresh.

Our Commitments to You

The VGGR Quality Pledges

Six promises we make to every customer, every day, without exception. These are not aspirations — they are operational standards.

No Preservatives

We use zero artificial preservatives, MSG, or synthetic flavour enhancers. Our food has a natural shelf life because it is made with real ingredients — and we're proud of that.

No Artificial Colors

The deep red-orange colour of our Ghee Roast comes entirely from Byadgi chilies. No food coloring, no turmeric overdose, no artificial hue enhancement. What you see is what nature and fire created.

Hygiene Certified

All VGGR kitchens are FSSAI registered and undergo daily internal hygiene audits. We conduct external third-party inspections quarterly. Our certificates are prominently displayed at every outlet.

Fresh Daily

We do not carry over food from the previous day. Every morning is a fresh start — new masala, new marination, new batch. If we run out before closing time, we close early. Simple.

100% Pure Ghee

Every batch of ghee we receive from our farm partners is tested in-house before use. We check for adulteration, impurities, and fat profile. Only batches that pass are used. No blends, no exceptions.

Handcrafted

Our masala is stone-ground by hand. Our marination is done by hand. Our roasting is monitored and adjusted by hand. No automated production lines touch the core of our cooking — and never will.
