Common Questions

Which areas do you deliver to?
We deliver to major parts of Central/South/East Delhi and Gurugram. If you are within a 8-10km radius of our kitchens, you should be able to find us on Swiggy or Zomato.
How long does delivery take?
Our average preparation time is 15-20 minutes, and delivery takes another 15-25 minutes depending on traffic and your distance from our kitchen.
Can I track my order?
Yes! Since all our orders are processed through Swiggy and Zomato, you get real-time GPS tracking from the moment the food leaves our kitchen to your doorstep.
What if my food arrives cold?
We use premium insulated packaging to ensure your ghee roast arrives piping hot. In the rare event of an issue, please reach out to the delivery partner's support for immediate assistance.
Do you offer contactless delivery?
Absolutely. You can select the "Leave at door" or "Contactless delivery" option during checkout on both Swiggy and Zomato.
